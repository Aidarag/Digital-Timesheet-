Student Success Center Digital Timesheet

Phase 1 PRD

1\. Product Goal

Digitize the current Livingstone College Success Center paper timesheet while keeping the existing structure familiar.

Phase 1 will combine:

\* Hourly Timesheet  
\* Student Session & Progress Record

into one digital workflow.

⸻

2\. Primary User

Student Employee / Tutor

The tutor should be able to record work hours and tutoring-session information in one place.

⸻

3\. Employee Information

The form should include:

\* Employee Name  
\* Department Name  
\* Beginning Date  
\* Ending Date / Reporting Period

⸻

4\. Main Timesheet Table

Keep the original hourly timesheet structure and add the tutoring information.

Date	In	Out	In	Out	Total Daily Hours	Weekly Total	Student Name / ID	Skills / Assignment(s) Worked On	Progress Notes

The original two In / Out periods must remain.

⸻

5\. Automatic Hour Calculation

The system should automatically calculate:

\* Total Daily Hours  
\* Weekly Total  
\* Total Hours for the Reporting Period

Users should not manually calculate their hours.

⸻

6\. Student Selection

The Student Name / ID field should use a searchable selection instead of requiring the tutor to type the student’s information every time.

Example:

Select Student ▼

Selecting a student should automatically display their name and ID.

⸻

7\. Multiple Student Sessions

If a tutor assists multiple students during the same day, they should be able to select:

\+ Add Student Session

Each additional session should include:

\* Student Name / ID  
\* Skills / Assignment  
\* Progress Notes

The work-hour section for the day should stay unchanged.

⸻

8\. Weekly Structure

The timesheet should contain:

\* Week 1  
\* Week 2  
\* Week 3  
\* Week 4  
\* Week 5

Each week should display its own total.

Users should also be able to select:

\+ Add Week

if needed.

⸻

9\. Summary

At the bottom of the timesheet, display:

\* Week 1 Total  
\* Week 2 Total  
\* Week 3 Total  
\* Week 4 Total  
\* Week 5 Total  
\* Total Hours This Period

All totals should be calculated automatically.

⸻

10\. Signature & Submission

Phase 1 should include:

\* Employee Signature  
\* Date Signed  
\* Save Draft  
\* Submit Timesheet

⸻

11\. Basic Validation

The system should prevent:

\* Out time before In time  
\* Missing required fields  
\* Invalid second In / Out entries  
\* Manual editing of calculated totals

⸻

12\. Phase 1 Success

Phase 1 is complete when a student employee can:

1\. Enter their information.  
2\. Record work hours.  
3\. Add tutoring-session information.  
4\. Select students without repeatedly typing their information.  
5\. View automatically calculated totals.  
6\. Save the timesheet.  
7\. Sign and submit it digitally.
